How to play

Write the numbers from 1 to 100 on the 10×10 grid, one per cell.

  • Put the 1 wherever you like: tap any cell.
  • Each following number starts from the cell of the last number you wrote.
  • Horizontally or vertically you skip 2 cells and land on the 3rd.
  • Diagonally you skip 1 cell and land on the 2nd.
  • You can’t reuse an occupied cell or leave the grid.
  • If you run out of moves before 100 you lose; reach 100 and you win.

Controls

  • Moves: shows or hides the cells you can jump to.
  • Undo: removes the last number; you can undo as many times as you like.
  • New game: starts over (asks for confirmation during a game).
  • The game saves itself: close it, reopen it and carry on where you left off.

Keyboard

  • ← ↑ → ↓ to move around the grid, Enter or Space to write the number.
  • Ctrl+Z or U to undo, M to show/hide moves.

Can you always win?

Yes: wherever the 1 starts, there is at least one path that reaches 100. Finding it is another story.

Privacy

No cookies, no tracking, no external services. Your game, your best score and your language choice stay in this browser only. The language is picked from your browser settings, on your device: nothing is sent anywhere.
